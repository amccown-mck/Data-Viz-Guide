Chart chooser

Pick the chart from the question, not from habit · McKesson data-viz guide

1 Write the question the chart answers→ 2 Find its type below and start with the first choice→ 3 Run the sanity checks at the bottom→ 4 Follow the cookbook page for the build
More than ~12 categories?Sort horizontal bars by value, or group the tail into "Other".
Values clustered far from zero?Don't truncate bars: use a dot plot or show the gap to benchmark.
Two units over time?No dual axes: use two aligned charts, or index both to 100.
Many groups, same measure?Small multiples on a shared scale, focus group highlighted in each.
Pie with 8 slices?Use a sorted bar. Pies and donuts take 5 parts at most.
