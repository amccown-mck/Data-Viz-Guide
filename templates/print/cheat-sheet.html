Chart cheat sheet

McKesson data-viz guide · for decks, reports and workbooks

1. Every chart has the same anatomy

1
Two frontline job families lose about 1 in 4 people a year, well above the 19.9% enterprise rate
2
Voluntary attrition by job family, FY26 (%)
3 4
Voluntary attrition = voluntary exits ÷ average headcount.
Source: Workday HCM, as of 31 Mar 2026; U.S. regular employees
1Headline = the insight. A full sentence with the number, ≤ 2 lines, no final period. 2Subtitle = the measure: metric, unit, scope, period. Neutral. 3Chart: grey context, blue focus, direct labels, a labelled benchmark. 4Footnote + source: definition, system, as-of date, population.

2. Write the headline last, from the numbers

Who or what + verb + number + so what. If you can't write it, you don't have a finding yet.

LabelAttrition by job family VagueAttrition varies across job families InsightTwo frontline job families lose about 1 in 4 people a year

Recompute every "all", "most", "2x" and "n of m" from the data.

Pick the chart from the question

How big → sorted bar · Over time → line · Parts of a whole → 100% bar or waterfall · Spread → histogram / strip · Two measures → scatter · Flows → Sankey · One number → KPI. Full guide: chart-chooser.pdf

3. Grey first, then color the headline's point

Three highlight patterns

Focus (default): all grey, headline items blue
Uniform + accent: one series blue, the exception orange
Focus + accent: grey, blue group, one orange exception

At most 2 highlighted items. Bold the matching category label.

Categories (max 6, in this order)

One measure, light to dark

Gap to a benchmark (centered on 0)

Never

  • Red vs. green (use orange vs. blue), rainbow bars, or color as the only cue
  • 3D, shadows, gradients, borders, or a legend labels could replace
  • Truncated bars or dual axes

4. Check before you send

  • Headline is an insight; every number in it is true
  • Subtitle has metric, unit, scope and period
  • Grey context; ≤ 2 highlights, matching the headline
  • Values labelled; value axis, gridlines and legend deleted
  • Bars start at zero; Excel gap width 50%
  • Sorted by value or in a natural order
  • Units in the subtitle; true minus (−), explicit + on changes
  • Metric defined; source has system, date, population
  • No group under 10 people, even in hover text
  • Nothing overlaps; nothing below 9 pt; one left edge

On a slide

  • The slide title is the headline; ≤ 2 charts per slide
  • Build up complex charts in 2–5 steps: grey, then highlight, then callout
  • Use native, editable charts, not pictures of charts
