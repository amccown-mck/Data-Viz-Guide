Overview

How to use this guide

One set of rules, three ways in. The guide teaches the thinking; the starter kit makes the right answer the easy one in every tool we use.

Every chart we make should do one job: answer a business question so clearly that the reader knows what to do next. The rules here are written to be checked, by a reviewer or by an agent, before a chart leaves the team.

Inspired by the structure of a leading consulting firm's public data-visualization guide, rebuilt for McKesson's brand and for the work of Workforce Innovation.

The five steps behind every chart

1 Question. Write the question the chart answers in one sentence.

2 Chart. Pick the chart from the question type, not from habit.

3 Build. Grey first, then color only what the headline names.

4 Headline. Compute the finding, then write it as a sentence.

5 QA. Run the checklist and look at the rendered chart.
