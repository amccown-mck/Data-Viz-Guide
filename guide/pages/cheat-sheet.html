Cheat sheet

Check before you send

  • The headline is an insight, and every number in it is true
  • Subtitle: metric, unit, scope and period
  • Grey context; at most 2 highlights, matching the headline
  • Values labelled; value axis, gridlines and legend deleted
  • Bars start at zero; no dual axes
  • Sorted by value, or in a natural order
  • True minus (−), explicit + on changes, "pts" for point changes
  • Metric defined; source names system, date and population
  • No group under {{text:min_n}} people, even in hover text
  • Nothing overlaps or clips; nothing below 9 pt

2 · Write the headline last

Who or what + verb + number + so what. Compute every number from the data.

3 · Grey first, then color the point

{{html:cs_roles}}

At most 2 highlights. Orange and green only mean worse and better. Red, amber, green: status only, with a symbol and word.

4 · Type (Libre Franklin, 16:9 slide)

{{html:cs_type}}

One chart: the slide title is its headline. Two: each its own SemiBold insight headline.

5 · Pick the chart from the question

  • How big? Sorted bar
  • Made of what? 100% bar, waterfall
  • Over time? Line
  • Who moved? Slope, dumbbell
  • How spread? Strip, histogram
  • Related? Scatter
  • Flows? Sankey · Where? Map
  • One number? Big number

Full version: the chart chooser.

6 · Never

  • Red vs. green, rainbows, or color as the only cue
  • 3D, shadows, gradients, borders
  • Truncated bars or dual axes
  • A legend where labels fit
  • More than 2 charts on a slide
  • Pictures of charts: use native, editable charts
