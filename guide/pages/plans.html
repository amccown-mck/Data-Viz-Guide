Plans and status visuals show what happens when, who owns it and whether it is on track: the workplan pages of a steering-committee deck.

A roadmap puts a program's workstreams across fiscal quarters, with milestones and a today line. Status colors appear only in the status column and on missed milestones.

  • Flat, square bars: grey, blue for what the headline names
  • Status as a dot with its symbol, plus the word

When a roadmap is the wrong chart

  • Progress to one target: a big number or a progress bar
  • Comparing units on a measure: bars, never status colors
  • Tasks, dates and hand-offs day to day: keep them in the project plan

Status best practice

Red, amber and green are loud. Give them one job, and never let them work alone.

Never color alone. Every dot carries its symbol (✓ ! ✕ –) and its word: red and green are the most common color-vision conflict.

Status, not data. Status colors mark workstreams and milestones. A measure gets grey and blue, and orange only when it's worse.

Flat and square. Small dots and thin rules; no pills, rounded boxes, shadows or gradients.

Exceptions first. Off track, then at risk; the rest in one grey line.

Plans and status

Build plans and status

No tool has one button for this. The parts are simple: bars, small shapes, a table, and the status colors with their symbols and words.

color.md: status colors · tables.md

Before you send it

  • Status colors only on status: dots, missed milestones
  • Every dot has its symbol and its word
  • The roll-up matches the rows ({{text:pl_rollup}})
  • A dated today line; moved dates say "was"
  • One owner per row; the as-of date in the subtitle
PowerPoint

Tracker: Insert › Table; in the status column a small circle (Insert › Shapes › Oval, hold Shift) filled on track #00AF68, at risk #FFB500, off track #DA2103, not started #BFBFBF or complete #005A8C, with the symbol and word beside it. Gantt: a Stacked Bar of start and duration; start series Fill › No fill; Categories in reverse order; milestones and today line as shapes.

Excel

The same stacked-bar Gantt. In a tracker sheet, keep the status word in its own column and color a ● beside it with Conditional Formatting › New Rule › Use a formula to determine which cells to format (=$C2="Off track", font #DA2103). Skip icon sets: their red, yellow and green are Excel's, can't be changed, and carry no word.

Power BI

Table visual: a measure returning the status hex (SWITCH on the status), applied to a ● column with Cell elements › Font color › fx › Format style: Field value; the word in the next column. No native Gantt: use Deneb (Vega-Lite, with the kit's vega-lite-config.json) or an AppSource Gantt with the status colors set by hand.

Python

matplotlib ax.barh(y, end - start, left=start) or broken_barh for bars, "D" markers for milestones, ax.axvline for today; Plotly px.timeline. Colors, symbols and words from mv.STATUS; process stages from mv.STEPS.

s = mv.STATUS["off_track"]       # value "#DA2103", symbol "✕", label "Off track"
ax.text(x, y, f'{s["symbol"]} {s["label"]}', color=C.text, va="center")
mv.lint(fig)    # notes status colors: status only, symbol and word
HTML

A .mck-table whose status cell holds a small circle in var(--mck-status-off-track) (and -on-track, -at-risk, -not-started, -done), the symbol inside and the word beside. Gantt bars: Plotly horizontal bars with base set to the start date.

R

geom_linerange() or geom_segment() for bars, geom_point(shape = 23) for milestone diamonds; status colors from mck_status, stage colors from mck_steps.
