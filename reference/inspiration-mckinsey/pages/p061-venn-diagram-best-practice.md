## Intent
Dos and don'ts for Venn diagrams in three panels: the color logic for overlaps, the choice of shapes and layout, and where text goes.

## Guidance (paraphrased, every rule stated on the page)
- **Overlapping circles:** overlaps should never be shown lighter than the single sets, because that misrepresents them. Show overlaps in darker shades of the color.
- **Playing with Venn diagram layouts:** stick to regular shapes. Don't turn the Venn areas into irregular blobs, which produce unbalanced layouts. Aim for regular, organic layouts that keep a simple, clear hierarchy even when the diagram is complex.
- **Using text with Venn diagrams:** don't put long text inside the Venn areas, since it can hide the shading and is hard to read. Move descriptive text outside the diagram and connect it with subtle leader lines.

## Examples marked Recommended
- A 3-circle Venn (A, B, C) with bright blue single sets, royal/electric blue pairwise overlaps and a near-black navy center (ABC). The shading darkens as overlap increases.
- A 4-set Venn (A, B, C, D) made of regular, symmetric elongated ellipses/petals. The overlaps are labeled BC, BCD, ABC, ABCD, AC, BD, AD and so on. Several blue tints run from pale sky blue (single sets) to deep navy (ABCD center). The structure is complex but balanced and easy to read.
- A 3-circle Venn where the descriptive text sits outside the circles, with thin connector lines from the set labels (A, B, C) to small text blocks placed left, right and upper right. The text is right-aligned on the right side. The text sizes are too small to read in detail (illegible).

## Examples marked Try to avoid / Not recommended
- The "Avoid" example (pink/red x icon, labeled "Avoid" rather than "Try to avoid") is a 3-circle Venn where the single circles are dark navy, the pairwise overlaps are royal blue and the center ABC is the lightest blue. The overlap shading is inverted.
- A Venn of 4 irregular, morphed blob shapes (A, B, C, D) with odd overlaps labeled AB, BC, AD, CD. The result looks unbalanced and hard to parse.
- A 3-circle Venn with long description text crammed inside the circle areas. The text overlaps the shading and is illegible even at intended scale.

## Visual specifics observed
- Color system: 3 or more blue tones mapped to the level of overlap. Light/cyan (Active Blue) = one set, electric/royal blue = two sets, deep navy = three or more. The 4-set example adds a paler sky blue for single sets.
- Shapes have no outlines. Labels are small letters (A, B, C, AB, ABC...) inside the regions. The labels are dark on light regions and white/light on dark regions.
- Leader lines are thin, dark grey and horizontal.
- Layout: 3 white cards on a light grey page (2 on top, 1 at bottom left). Each card has a medium-weight sans title, two thumbnails, a thin divider rule, and verdict labels with icons. The left-hand verdicts use a navy "x" circle ("Try to avoid"), except the first panel, which uses a pink/red "x" circle labeled "Avoid". The right-hand verdicts use a bright blue check circle ("Recommended").
- Typography: the page title is a large serif on two lines, with a small bold sans eyebrow "Relationships" above it. The captions are small regular sans.

## Annotation & narrative patterns
- The thumbnails have no headlines. The guidance sits in the card titles and captions.
- The pattern is to keep annotations outside the shapes and connect them with subtle lines, which matches page 60.
