## Intent
Shows how charts can sit inside editorial print layouts built on two- and three-column grids, integrated with body text rather than as full-page features. Use for reports/articles where charts accompany running copy.

## Guidance (paraphrased, every rule stated on the page)
- Left-rail subhead: "Editorial charts".
- Charts can be placed within an editorial layout and should fit two-column and three-column grid structures.
- (No other explicit rules; the page is a mock-up of a printed double spread.)

## Visual specifics observed
- Color roles: stacked area uses a blue family, six series — legend squares approx. Fiat light lavender, Ford teal/steel blue, Volkswagen light cyan, Vauxhall royal blue, Toyota bright blue, Kia dark navy/black. Donut: dark navy largest segment, bright cyan and royal blue segments, grey for "Not sure".
- Axes: area chart has light y-axis tick labels, no heavy gridlines, baseline at 0; x-axis two-digit year labels.
- Labels: legend placed at left of area chart (vertical list with square markers); donut legend at right with small dot markers and right-aligned values. Annotations placed above the chart aligned to the relevant x-range (thin horizontal rules mark each range), with bold short title + 2–3 lines explanation.
- Donut: thin ring, center text label.
- Big numbers: large serif numerals with superscript-sized % sign, small sans caption beneath.
- Typography: section eyebrow sans "Format"; page title serif "Print"; editorial headings serif; chart headlines bold sans; subtitles regular sans; captions/footnotes very small sans grey.

## Annotation & narrative patterns
- Insight-style chart headlines with descriptive subtitles (metric, geography, date range, unit/sample size in parentheses).
- Event annotations titled with short phrases ("The crash", "Turning the corner").
- Footnote + source lines set small under each chart and at page bottom.
- Big-number callouts used as standalone data highlights in the text column.
