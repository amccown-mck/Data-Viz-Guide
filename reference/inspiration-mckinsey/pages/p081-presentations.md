## Intent
Shows how data visualizations are applied inside presentation documents (slide decks). Main message: use build-up slides that reveal content step by step to keep the audience engaged.

## Guidance (paraphrased, every rule stated on the page)
- Data visualization works well in presentation documents.
- To keep viewers engaged, build charts up over successive slides and reveal content progressively.
