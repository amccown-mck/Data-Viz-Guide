## Intent
Defines simplified, high-contrast palettes for social media assets. They can be used in place of the full functional colour range when making simple, platform-specific visuals for channels such as Twitter, Facebook and Instagram. The palettes are built around the brand blues to strengthen brand recognition and keep data visualization consistent across social channels. They are not meant for complex charts that need the full functional palette.

## Guidance (paraphrased, every rule stated on the page)
- Social media assets may use simple three-tone palettes (a background plus one or two foreground colours) instead of the full functional colour range.
- These combinations are high-contrast and centre on the palette's blues, to support brand recognition and consistency across social channels.
- They are recommended for simpler visualizations made specifically for Twitter, Facebook, Instagram and similar platforms.

## Visual specifics observed
- Colour roles: the background is the dominant field colour. The one to three foreground colours are for data marks and text. White or deep navy provides contrast, and light/electric blue is the accent.
- Colour count: at most three tones per palette (one background and up to two or three foreground swatches). Panel 1 shows three foregrounds, the others show two.
- Each swatch is a solid filled circle with a label underneath: bold name, then hex, then RGB value.
- Each panel's top-left corner carries the background colour's name, hex and RGB in the same three-line format.
- Note the naming mismatch: Electric Blue is written #1f40e6, but its RGB is given as (0, 45, 190). #1f40e6 is actually RGB(31, 64, 230), so the two values are inconsistent on the page.
- Typography: the page title is a large serif. The "Alternative colors" subhead is bold sans. Body text and the colour specs are small sans. The "Examples" cross-reference is in italics.
