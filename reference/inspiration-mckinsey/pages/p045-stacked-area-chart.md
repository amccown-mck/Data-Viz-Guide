## Intent
Introduces the stacked area chart for trend-over-time analysis. Each series is stacked on top of the previous one, so the chart shows how several variables change over intervals while also conveying total magnitude and each segment's contribution to the whole. Suited to part-to-whole-over-time stories.

## Guidance (paraphrased, every rule stated on the page)
- A stacked area chart layers multiple series, each starting where the previous series ends; useful for comparing several variables changing over time.
- The fill between segments and the x-axis emphasises magnitude, letting viewers read how much each segment contributes to the total.
- Segment colors follow the order of the firm's qualitative palette, beginning with the deep/dark blue at the bottom of the stack.
- Use lightly shaded regions of the chart to highlight the periods tied to the key insights.
- The stacked segments make this chart a suitable part-to-whole device for trend analysis.

## Examples marked Recommended
- Single worked example (not explicitly marked; presented as the model chart): stacked area chart of UK new car sales by brand, 2004–2017, with two highlighted periods and two callouts — demonstrates palette order, highlight shading, and insight annotation.
