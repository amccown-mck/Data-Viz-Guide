## Intent
Catalogue of four bar chart variants for comparing magnitudes, each with a one-line note on when it fits: horizontal bars for long labels or narrow layouts, grouped bars for several series per category, butterfly for comparing two groups, and diverging bars for mixed positive/negative values.

## Guidance (paraphrased, every rule stated on the page)
- Horizontal bar chart: easier to label than vertical bars and works better in narrow layouts such as mobile.
- Grouped bar chart: use when two or more data series appear side by side and are clustered under shared categories on a single axis.
- Butterfly chart: places two series back-to-back to show the difference between two groups measured on the same parameters. To emphasize the negative side (bars extending left), the brand "Watermelon" color may be applied.
- Diverging bar chart: a standard bar chart that handles both positive and negative values. To emphasize the negatives (bars extending down), the "Watermelon" color may be applied.

## Visual specifics observed
- Color roles: dark navy is the default fill for single-series bars (horizontal, butterfly, diverging). In the grouped chart, three series use dark navy, a light/cyan blue, and a saturated royal/electric blue, one color per series in the same order within each category. The Watermelon accent is mentioned in the text but not shown.
- Horizontal bar: category labels A–H on the left, value labels (e.g., 219, 280, 388) placed directly at bar ends, no gridlines and no value axis. A single "0" marks the baseline under the bars. Bars are in alphabetical category order, not sorted by value. The bars are thick, with gaps about half a bar height.
- Grouped bar: y-axis ticks 0/50/100, very faint horizontal gridlines at 50 and 100, and a thin baseline at 0. Category labels A–D sit under each cluster. Bars within a cluster touch or nearly touch, with larger gaps between clusters. The chart has no legend and no value labels.
- Butterfly: column headers "Disagree" (left) and "Agree" (right), with category labels A–G in a central gutter between the two sides. Each side has its own axis (100/50/0 on the left, 0/50/100 on the right) with very faint vertical gridlines, and both sides use the same navy fill.
- Diverging bar: vertical columns A–G with a thin zero baseline labeled "0". Two bars (C, F) extend below zero. The chart has no other axis ticks or gridlines, and the category labels sit below the plot.
- Layout: a left rail holds the section eyebrow and title. The right side is a 2x2 grid of white cards on a light grey page, each card with a bold sans subtitle and a short regular-weight description.
- Typography: the page title "Bar chart variants" is a large serif (two lines). The eyebrow "Magnitude" is a small bold sans, and the card titles are small bold sans. Descriptions and axis labels use a light/regular sans at small size.
