# Run: 2026-10-18, Claude.ai, Opus 5.5, skill zip with the wheel (v1.1.0)

Dataset: a fresh one, not in the repo at the time (distribution-center overtime, 8 sites, one with 7 associates, network average 5.9 hours; now `../overtime_by_dc.csv`). Prompt: "/mck-chart Which distribution centers are running the most overtime, and how far above the network average are they? I need one exec-ready chart with a headline, then turn it into a PowerPoint slide." Then: five alternative storylines; then the chosen one as a slide with speaker notes. The bundle the model exported (transcript, charts, two .pptx, scripts, its own review notes) is outside the repo.

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| 1. Setup | `check_env.py` installed mckviz 1.1.0 from `assets/python/` on the first try, Libre Franklin registered. `mv.lint` ran on every chart. |
| 2. Chart family | Turn 1: diverging bars (`mv.diverging_bars`, worse above), with the network line labelled in place. Acceptable (sorted bar with a line was the first choice). Options: ranked bar of derived hours, dumbbell of shares, waterfall of excess hours, bubble scatter, KPI ring. All distinct question types; what the data cannot support was listed (trend, cost, causes). |
| 3. Focus and colors | Above-average sites orange (worse), below in blue; Aurora bold navy. Options: at most 2 in blue, rest grey; no green; waterfall totals navy. Pass. |
| 4. Headline | "Three of seven distribution centers run above the 5.9-hour network average, led by Aurora at +2.0 hours a week." Numbers match (Aurora +2.0, 3 of 7). Leading number spelled out. Option headlines recomputed in code (47,147 hours, 49%, 53%, 4,748 excess). Pass. |
| 5. Suppression | The 7-associate pilot was suppressed with `mv.suppress_small`, footnoted on every chart, absent from labels, notes and the headline. Pass. |
| 6. Source line | "Source: [confirm source and as-of date]; associates at 7 distribution centers" throughout; period left out of subtitles; flagged in the hand-back. Nothing invented. Pass. |
| 7. QA | Lint plus render-and-look. The model fixed: "+2" vs "+2.0" in the headline, "Three of 7", a mid-word wrap in the waterfall ("Shepherdsvill/e"), an unbolded dumbbell focus row, a benchmark label on a bubble. Lint noise it reported: `orange-semantic` INFO on diverging bars, `bars-from-zero` WARN on the floored waterfall despite the note. |
| 8. PowerPoint | Native pptxgenjs charts from `assets/office/pptxgenjs-theme.js`: title placeholder 26 pt Bold McKesson Blue, labels and lines as shapes over a pinned plot area, validated and rendered through LibreOffice, speaker notes with the overlay caveat and the source placeholder. The stacked waterfall needed `dataLabelPosition: "ctr"` (the preset's `outEnd` is invalid on stacked charts). Pass. |
| 9. Findings | Ten friction points, all real: stacked preset, no pptxgenjs waterfall recipe, waterfall wrap mid-word, no `focus=` on `mv.dumbbell`, lint INFO on designed orange, lint WARN on a noted floor, `mv.signed` default decimals, per-point label colors impossible in pptxgenjs, Bold vs Medium for slide focus labels, subtitle INFO when the period is unknown. |

Verdict: pass on every check. The install path, suppression, placeholder source and native slide were the four things the first test failed, and all four held. Fixed from the findings the same day: word-safe wrap, `focus=` on `dumbbell`, lint downgrades the floor warning when the footnote mentions the break, stacked presets in the pptxgenjs theme, and the office-setup notes (stacked labels, per-point label colors, Bold focus labels). Open: a pptxgenjs waterfall recipe, lint skipping designed orange, `mv.signed` decimals, the subtitle rule when the period is unknown.
